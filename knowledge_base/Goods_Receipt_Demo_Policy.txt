SYNTHETIC DEMO POLICY - NOT AN ORGANISATIONAL POLICY

1. Goods Receipt submissions should contain evidence appropriate to the transaction type.
2. For recurring services, the Purchase Order, applicable Invoice Instruction and supplier invoice are the core documents for financial validation.
3. Delivery or acceptance evidence may be required for a first payment or where the applicable procedure requires evidence of service commencement/acceptance.
4. For subsequent recurring invoices, delivery/acceptance evidence may be optional where the applicable procedure does not require re-submission.
5. The annual Invoice Instruction allocation should be checked against the current invoice and cumulative utilisation where that information is available.
6. Variances should be highlighted for Finance review rather than automatically rejected.
7. Finance retains the final decision to approve, return or process the Goods Receipt.
